About this service

Teens are growing, testing limits and working out who they are. Therapy at Mindful Mountain gives them a space to be heard, with caregivers involved as partners in the process.

We draw on trauma-informed and attachment-focused approaches, EMDR, play therapies and a full teen DBT program, tailored to each teen's needs, strengths and goals.

Approaches we use

  • Trauma-informed therapy
  • Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)
  • EMDR
  • Attachment-focused approaches
  • Collaborative Problem Solving (CPS)
  • Caregiver and family coaching
