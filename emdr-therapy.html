About this service

EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) is one of the trauma-informed approaches our team offers for children, teens and adults who are working through difficult experiences.

Several of our providers are trained in EMDR, including Terra Alagna and Reagan Costello-White, and Decca Knight is a Certified EMDR Therapist with additional specializations.
