DBT Program for Teens & Caregivers

A comprehensive DBT program that helps teens build skills and helps caregivers learn how to support them.

Ages 13-19Tuesdays 5:30-7:30 PMIn person, RoanokePrivate pay only
Request an appointment

About this service

When a teen experiences intense emotions, the challenges often affect the entire family. Our program combines weekly individual therapy, a weekly DBT skills coaching group for teens and caregivers, and between-session phone coaching.

Learning DBT skills together gives teens and caregivers a shared language to use at home. Phone coaching is for skills support in the moment. It is not an emergency or crisis service.

Skills teens and caregivers practice

  • Managing big emotions
  • Staying mindful
  • Coping with distress
  • Working through conflict
  • Improving communication
  • Strengthening relationships
  • Responding rather than reacting

Providers

  • Ryan White, DO, Psychiatrist
  • Terra Alagna, LCSW, Therapist

Insurance varies by provider. See insurance

What to expect

1. Weekly individual therapy

A private space to work on personal goals and apply DBT skills to your teen's own experiences.

2. Weekly skills coaching group

Tuesdays from 5:30 to 7:30 PM. The first hour is peer support, and the second hour is DBT skills practice for teens and caregivers together.

3. Between-session phone coaching

Reach your skills coach to apply DBT skills when real-life situations happen.

Program commitment

Teens begin with four individual sessions to build the relationship and confirm readiness before joining the group. The group is private pay only and is not billed to insurance. Contact us for current availability and fees.
