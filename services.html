Services for children, teens and families

Therapy, psychiatry and family support, matched to what each child and family needs.

Psychiatry & Medication Management

Thoughtful psychiatric care for children, teens, adults and families, individualized to the person and not simply a diagnosis or a prescription.

Teen Therapy

Developmentally appropriate therapy for teens and young adults that respects their voice and involves their family.

Child Therapy

Therapy that meets children where they are, with play as a way to say what words cannot.

DBT Program

A comprehensive DBT program that helps teens build skills and helps caregivers learn how to support them.

EMDR Therapy

Trauma-informed EMDR therapy for children, teens and families.

Theraplay

Building connection, safety and stronger relationships between children and their caregivers.

Child-Centered Play Therapy

A safe, child-led space to express and process experiences through play.

Family Therapy

Support for the whole family, including families affected by adoption, foster care and trauma.
