Child-Centered Play Therapy

A safe, child-led space to express and process experiences through play.

ChildrenPlay-based
Request an appointment

About this service

Child-centered play therapy gives children a therapeutic space to express and process their experiences through play, especially when words are hard to find.

It is different from Theraplay, where the caregiver actively participates. Your therapist can help decide which approach, or combination of approaches, is best for your child.

What it supports

  • Expressing big emotions
  • Working through difficult experiences
  • Building confidence and coping skills
  • Strengthening emotional understanding
