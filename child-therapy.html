About this service

Children communicate and grow in different ways at different ages. For younger children, therapy often includes child-centered play therapy, which gives them a way to communicate and work through emotions through play.

Caregivers are important partners. Through parent consultations, coaching and family sessions when appropriate, we help caregivers understand their child's needs and support progress at home.

Approaches we use

  • Child-centered play therapy
  • Theraplay
  • EMDR
  • Trauma-informed care
  • Attachment-focused approaches
  • Caregiver coaching and support
