Holistic family wellness

The name Mindful Mountain is a nod to the beautiful mountains of Virginia and a reminder of the mountains we are all climbing in our lives.

Who we are

Mindful Mountain Wellness Center is a holistic mental health center based in the Roanoke Valley.

Every provider at Mindful Mountain is not only a mental health professional but also a parent, with first-hand experience in foster care, adoption and trauma-informed care.

Our collective journey as parents and caregivers inspired us to establish Mindful Mountain, because families need comprehensive mental health care during challenging times.

Meet the team

Our approach

Connection comes first

A trusting therapeutic relationship creates space to explore difficult feelings. We bring warmth, curiosity and compassion, and respect your pace.

Trauma-informed

We consider how past experiences may affect your sense of safety and relationships, while recognizing the strengths you bring.

Supporting children and families

Children grow in different ways at different ages. Caregivers are partners through consultations, coaching and family sessions.

You don't have to figure it out alone.

Tell us what is going on and we will help you understand your options.
