About Reagan Costello-White

Reagan is passionate about supporting children, teens, young adults and families during challenging times. She draws on her experience with foster and adoptive families to provide compassionate and effective support for those parenting children from hard places.

Reagan knows every child is different and has unique needs. Her goal is to help families navigate difficult times and find what works for their unique child. She incorporates play therapy, EMDR and Dialectical Behavior Therapy to foster healing and strengthen attachment.
