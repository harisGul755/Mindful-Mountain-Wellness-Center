When therapy and psychiatry work together

Mental health isn't one-dimensional, and treatment doesn't have to be either. Therapy can focus on emotional regulation, relationships, trauma and behavior, while psychiatric care addresses symptoms that may benefit from medication or further evaluation.

Our goal isn't to put you in a treatment category. It's to understand what you need and help you find the right support.
