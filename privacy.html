Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Information we collect

When you use this website or contact us, we may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number and email address, and basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited.

Appointment requests

Appointment requests are submitted through a third-party form (Jotform). Please share only what is needed to request an appointment. Do not use the form or email for emergencies or to send detailed clinical information.

How we use information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, schedule appointments, and improve this website.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies or analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help operate this website or our forms, or when required by law.

Your health information

Information about your care is protected under federal and state law and is described in our Notice of Privacy Practices, available from our office.

Children

This website is intended for parents, caregivers and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through this site.
