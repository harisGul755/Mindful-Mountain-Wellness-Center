About Ryan White

Dr. White is a board-certified child and adolescent psychiatrist who enjoys partnering with families to heal from trauma and mental health disorders. He has extensive experience working with foster and adoptive families and children who are neurodivergent.

He draws on Collaborative and Proactive Solutions (CPS), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) and play therapy in his sessions with youth and families. Dr. White is also passionate about treating substance use in adolescents and adults using medication assisted treatment (MAT).

Training and specialties

  • Board-certified child and adolescent psychiatrist
  • Theraplay Trained - Level 1
  • Medication assisted treatment (MAT)
