About this service

Sometimes therapy is the right place to start. Sometimes a psychiatric evaluation provides helpful information and more treatment options. And sometimes the most effective approach includes both.

Psychiatry is a medical specialty focused on understanding and treating mental health conditions and the ways they affect emotions, thoughts, behavior, relationships and daily functioning. An evaluation looks at the whole picture, including symptoms, developmental, medical and family history, medications, relationships and school or work functioning.

Concerns we may work with

  • Anxiety
  • Depression and mood concerns
  • Emotional dysregulation
  • Behavioral challenges
  • Trauma-related symptoms
  • ADHD and related concerns
  • Neurodivergence
  • Family and relationship difficulties
  • Significant changes in mood or behavior

What to expect

Session one: caregiver interview

Parents or caregivers meet the psychiatrist without the child present, so they can speak openly about concerns, history and school experiences. It also protects the child from hearing adults discuss sensitive concerns about them.

Session two: meeting your child

The psychiatrist builds rapport, learns about your child in their own words, and adapts the conversation and activities to their developmental level.

After the evaluation

The psychiatrist discusses impressions and recommendations with the family. An evaluation does not automatically mean medication will be recommended.
